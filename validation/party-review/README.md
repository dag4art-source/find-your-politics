# Find Your Politics - External party review package

Date prepared: 2026-08-20

Purpose: invite all eight parliamentary parties to review the factual representation of their own publicly stated policy positions in Find Your Politics.

## What we are asking parties to review

Only this question:

> Does the attached 27-dimension representation fairly reflect your party's current publicly stated national policy positions for the 2026 election?

We are **not** asking parties to approve the scoring model, endorse the app, or agree with how another party is represented.

## Equal-treatment rule

Every party receives:
- the same cover message
- the same 27 dimensions
- the same -100 to +100 directional scale
- the same confidence definitions
- the same correction rule

A requested correction is accepted when it is supported by current, publicly accessible, official national party material. The evidence rule is identical for all eight parties.

Corrections are logged. They are not silently overwritten.

## Score bands

- -100 to -75: very strong toward pole A
- -74 to -35: clearly toward pole A
- -34 to +34: mixed, moderate or ambiguous
- +35 to +74: clearly toward pole B
- +75 to +100: very strong toward pole B

Exact numbers inside a band are modelling judgements, not measured facts. For external review, the most important question is whether the direction and approximate strength are fair.

## Confidence labels

- H = current official material directly supports the direction and approximate band
- M = direction is supported, but exact strength is interpretive or the dimension is composite
- L = evidence is indirect, mixed or unusually broad; these are priority review cells

## Requested reply format

For each disputed coordinate, please provide:

1. Dimension name
2. Current score you believe is inaccurate
3. Suggested direction or approximate band
4. A current official national source URL
5. Optional short explanation

Example:

> Dimension: nuclear_energy
> Current representation: +55
> Suggested representation: +75 to +100
> Official source: [URL]
> Comment: Our 2026 position explicitly calls for major new nuclear expansion.

## Public transparency rule

After the review round, the project may state only factual participation information, for example:

> All eight parliamentary parties were invited to review the factual representation of their publicly stated positions under the same evidence rule.

If a party responds, the response and any accepted corrections should be recorded internally. No lack of response should be described as approval.
