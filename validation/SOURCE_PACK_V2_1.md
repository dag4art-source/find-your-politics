## Source hygiene rule going forward

1. Prefer a current 2026 manifesto or national programme.
2. Prefer a current official national policy page when it exists and resolves consistently.
3. If a party restructures its site, use its current national policy index or 2026 manifesto rather than preserving a dead deep link.
4. Source-key URL corrections do not by themselves change a coordinate. A score change requires new substantive evidence.
5. Before the public release freeze, run a link check on every source key and record the date of the check.
