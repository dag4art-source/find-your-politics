## How to read this file

Each cell is `score / confidence / source key`.

Confidence:
- **H** = current official material directly addresses the axis and direction is clear.
- **M** = current official material supports the direction, but the exact strength is an editorial judgement or the axis combines related policies.
- **L** = source support is indirect, mixed, or the axis itself needs more granular evidence. These coordinates should be challenged first in external review.

The score is not a measured fact. It places the party on a directional band from -100 to +100 so the same comparison rule can be applied to all parties.

## Important interpretation notes

1. **Confidence does not mean scientific certainty.** It means confidence that official current party material supports the direction and approximate band.
2. **Low-confidence cells remain in the model only when removing the whole dimension would lose useful information.** They are priority targets for party review.
3. **Some axes are inherently composite.** `welfare_private_profit`, `social_values`, `state_scope`, `tax_wealth_property` and aspects of `housing_supply` remain the least clean conceptual dimensions.
4. **Liberalerna on welfare profit is deliberately near neutral/slightly restrictive in V2.** The party still supports school choice but its 2026 position explicitly rejects profit-driven schools. A single welfare-profit axis therefore cannot place L as strongly pro-profit without misrepresenting current school policy.
5. **Socialdemokraterna on nuclear is materially revised.** Current official policy allows new nuclear at existing nuclear sites and supports life extensions and power uprates, while differing from the right on the financing model.
6. **Centerpartiet on citizenship is materially revised.** Current 2026 proposals combine stricter general requirements with a faster route for strongly established contributors.
7. **Moderaterna on skilled migration is materially revised.** Current policy combines very restrictive asylum policy with explicit routes/exemptions for highly qualified and shortage labour.

## External-review rule

When a party disputes a coordinate, a change is accepted only if the party points to current, publicly accessible official national material. The same evidentiary rule applies to all eight parties. Corrections and source changes should be logged rather than silently overwritten.
